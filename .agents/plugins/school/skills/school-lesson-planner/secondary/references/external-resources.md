# Reliable External Resources

Use web search only for supplementary learning resources and only when they add clear pedagogical value.

## Preferred source order

1. Official government and public-institution websites
2. Universities and recognized research institutes
3. Official museums, archives, laboratories, observatories, libraries, and scientific organizations
4. Peer-reviewed academic publications
5. Recognized international education and science organizations
6. High-quality open educational resources with clear authorship

## Appropriate resource types

- video materials;
- virtual museum or archive collections;
- laboratory demonstrations;
- scientific datasets and visualizations;
- academic or review articles;
- official mathematics, biology, chemistry, physics, geography, history, language, and arts resources.

## For each recommended resource, provide

- title;
- organization/source;
- type;
- intended lesson stage;
- pedagogical purpose;
- language;
- access conditions;
- direct citation/link.

## Exclusions

Do not use random blogs, anonymous materials, unreliable answer sites, or commercial resources as primary evidence. Do not recommend a paid or account-required resource without a free, accessible alternative.
