# Internal Quality Audit

Use Form 1 in the bundled `lesson-quality-standard.docx` as the authoritative framework for a KSP before the lesson. Never score a plan with Form 2 or claim classroom outcomes that have not been observed.

## Form 1: KSP design quality

Score each criterion `0-2`: `2` = concrete and aligned; `1` = partial or formal; `0` = absent or contradictory. Maximum: 28.

1. Alignment with the curriculum.
2. Measurable lesson objective.
3. Success evidence expressed through the selected template's assessment-criteria field; do not add a duplicate success-criteria field to the bundled form.
4. Coherence: objective -> task -> learner result -> assessment.
5. Attention to the starting level.
6. Logic of stages.
7. Cognitive activity.
8. Learner independence.
9. Formative assessment.
10. Actionable feedback.
11. Differentiation and accessibility.
12. Resources and feasibility.
13. Lesson completion with demonstration of the result.
14. Teacher reflection and a next-lesson adjustment.

Interpretation: `24-28` methodologically coherent; `18-23` workable with targeted revision; `11-17` key elements present but weakly connected; `0-10` redesign required. This is diagnostic, not an official NAO attestation scale.

## Mandatory audit questions

### Interview integrity
- Were all eleven mandatory fields obtained from the teacher or attached documents and explicitly confirmed?
- Does the conversation contain a real stop-and-wait interview turn before lesson design?
- Is every professional, class, curriculum, and lesson parameter traceable to user-provided evidence?
- Are exact learning-objective codes and wording preserved verbatim?
- If an annual KTP was available in the Project, was the curriculum section taken verbatim from the uniquely matched KTP row instead of being asked again or invented?
- Was demonstration mode kept interactive rather than completed with synthetic answers?

### Alignment
- Are official learning objectives reproduced exactly?
- Are lesson objectives observable, achievable, and derived from them?
- Does every major task produce evidence for a stated objective?
- Do assessment criteria and descriptors match the required evidence?
- Does the methodological appendix contain exactly the five required items derived from the exact objective and lesson topic: subject content, factual knowledge, subject-specific skills, cognitive skills, and final evidence of learning?
- Is every claimed skill traceable to the objective, topic, or an actual lesson task, with learner evidence?
- Is the selected methodology shown through a concrete mechanism, learner action, evidence, and teacher response rather than merely named?
- If a specialized method name or acronym is used, is it explained briefly in plain language, with its lesson function and recommendation status?

### Learner cognition
- Do learners perform the main intellectual work?
- Do tasks require appropriate thinking rather than only reproduction?
- Is there a meaningful opportunity to apply feedback or improve work?

### Assessment
- Is prior understanding checked?
- Is progress checked for most learners, not only volunteers?
- Is feedback specific about success and next step?
- Is the teacher prepared to adjust instruction based on evidence?
- Are lesson objectives and assessment criteria each formatted as separate true bullet items in the selected language template?

### Inclusion and differentiation
- Are access, support, and ways to demonstrate the result adapted while the content goal remains unchanged unless reduction is normatively and pedagogically justified?
- Are supports available without stigmatization?
- Does extension deepen thinking?
- Are multiple valid participation or response formats available where needed?

### Feasibility
- Does time add up exactly?
- Is the number of activities realistic?
- Are transitions and resource requirements manageable?
- Is there a non-digital alternative when technology is used?

### Template fidelity
- If a user template was supplied, was that exact file used as the source?
- Are all section labels, their order, merged cells, table geometry, margins, and page orientation preserved?
- Is every original section completed or left with a clear administrative placeholder?
- Was the methodological appendix added only after the complete source form?
- Were the source and completed DOCX rendered and visually compared page by page?

### Safety and integrity
- Are dignity, physical and psychological safety protected?
- Are sources and facts reliable?
- Are personal data protected?
- Is AI absent unless requested, and is human verification retained?

Treat safety, non-discrimination, subject accuracy, device-independent access, personal-data protection, and responsible AI use as gates outside the 28-point score. A failed gate cannot be offset by a high score.

## Critical failure conditions

Do not deliver a new KSP until corrected if any of these is true:
- the mandatory interview is incomplete, unconfirmed, simulated, or answered by the assistant;
- any teacher, class, curriculum, or lesson parameter was invented;
- exact learning objectives were not supplied by the teacher or an attached authoritative document;
- a supplied template was replaced, restructured, or cannot be visually matched to the completed DOCX;
- no clear objective or no evidence of learning;
- the objective, task, learner result, and assessment do not form a coherent chain;
- the lesson has no planned completion in which learners demonstrate the result linked to the objective;
- teacher/technology/AI performs the main intellectual work;
- a learner is excluded because of disability, language, pace, socioeconomic status, or device access;
- unsafe, humiliating, discriminatory, or stigmatizing content appears;
- a significant subject error remains;
- an automated system makes the final assessment decision;
- personal or sensitive data are exposed;
- a mandatory activity requires a personal account, paid service, or device without an equivalent alternative.

## Visible audit result

By default, show only a concise statement that the audit was completed and identify any important design assumption. Show a detailed checklist only for an open lesson, formal review, attestation context, or explicit user request.
