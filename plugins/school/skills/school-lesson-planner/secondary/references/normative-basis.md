# Normative Basis and Language Forms

## Status

Base the KSP structure on Order of the Minister of Education and Science of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 6 April 2020 No. 130, registered under No. 20317, as amended. The order regulates the list and forms of documents maintained by teachers. The KSP form was reflected in amendments including Order No. 382 dated 27 August 2022.

The legal record was checked on 3 August 2026. The Adilet record also lists a 2026 amendment: Order of the Acting Minister of Education dated 15 July 2026 No. 194-НҚ, registered on 17 July 2026 under No. 39321.

Before producing a document for official use, check the current consolidated text and the relevant form in Adilet. Do not call a generated document an “official template” unless its conformity with the current consolidated text has been verified at generation time. Refer to it as a form based on Order No. 130 when current verification is unavailable.

## Mandatory minimum reproduced in the open guidance

Preserve these elements:

- curriculum section;
- teacher name;
- lesson date;
- grade and present/absent counts;
- lesson topic;
- learning objectives according to the curriculum;
- lesson objectives;
- lesson progress: stage/time, teacher actions, learner actions, assessment, resources.

For the bundled Order No. 130 forms, assessment criteria may be added as a useful methodological field. Clearly treat it as an addition rather than claiming that it is part of the mandatory minimum reproduced above. Never add an expected-results field to a bundled form. Preserve every original field, including learner-outcome fields, when a user-supplied template is the designated source.

## Language status

- `assets/ksp-template-kk.docx` uses the Kazakh labels reproduced in the National Academy guidance.
- `assets/ksp-template-ru.docx` uses the Russian labels reproduced in the same guidance.
- `assets/ksp-template-en.docx` is a controlled working translation for English-medium planning. It is not an official English version of Order No. 130.

Use one language consistently. Do not mix labels from different templates.
