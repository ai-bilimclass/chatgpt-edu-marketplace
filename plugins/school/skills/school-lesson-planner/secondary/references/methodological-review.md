# Methodological Review of an Existing KSP

Use this mode only when the user explicitly asks to review or check an existing lesson plan.

## Review procedure

1. Extract the stated learning objectives, lesson objectives, tasks, assessment, timing, differentiation, and resources.
2. Compare the plan with Form 1 of the bundled lesson quality standard: 14 criteria on the 0-2 scale plus the mandatory gates.
3. Distinguish observed evidence in the document from assumptions.
4. Identify strengths, risks, gaps, and critical failures.
5. Give prioritized, actionable recommendations.
6. Do not use Form 2, its 0-3 scale, or claims about learner progress unless the user also provides post-lesson observational evidence.
7. Rewrite the KSP only when the user asks for an improved version.

## Review output

1. **Brief conclusion**
2. **Strong aspects**
3. **Methodological risks**
4. **Misalignments and missing elements**
5. **Priority improvements**
6. **Critical-condition check**
7. **Optional revised version**

If the user requests a score, report the Form 1 total out of 28 with the diagnostic band and identify any failed gate separately. Do not assign a punitive rating or fabricate classroom evidence that is not present in the document.
