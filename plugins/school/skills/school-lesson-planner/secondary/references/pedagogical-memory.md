# Pedagogical Memory and Reflection

## Purpose

Create continuity across a sequence of lessons inside the current conversation. Use only anonymized, instructionally relevant information.

## Pedagogical-memory record

Maintain a compact record with these fields:

- Teacher experience, attestation level / qualification category, and class size when supplied
- Subject, grade, class/group label if supplied
- Language of instruction
- Class strengths
- Current misconceptions or unfinished learning
- Learners/groups needing support
- Learners/groups needing extension
- Effective methods and resources
- Methods/resources to reconsider
- Evidence obtained in the last lesson
- Next instructional step selected by the teacher
- Methods recently used

Summarize rather than storing long transcripts.

## Post-lesson reflection trigger

Use this mode immediately when the teacher says or selects, for example:
- «📊 Провести педагогическую рефлексию»
- «Проведи педагогическую рефлексию урока»
- «Сабақ бойынша рефлексия жүргіз»
- “Conduct a post-lesson reflection.”

Do not respond with a description of the mode. Ask the three core questions in one message and wait for the teacher's answers.

Also invoke this reflection before designing the next lesson for the same class when no reflection evidence from the previous lesson is present. In that case, reflection is a required gate unless the teacher explicitly skips it or states that no prior-lesson data are available.

## Adaptive three-question reflection model

Always ask exactly three questions. Preserve the three core meanings below, but adapt or rephrase the wording to the subject, grade, class characteristics, previous lesson, and known barriers. Do not ask for information already supplied. Ask in Kazakh, Russian, or English according to the teacher's language or the current KSP language.

### Core meanings

1. Evidence of learning: what specific learner answers, actions, or products demonstrate attainment of the learning objective; who attained it fully or partially; what barriers affected attainment.
2. Impact of lesson design: how teaching approaches, differentiation, feedback, classroom environment, and time organization influenced engagement and learning; what worked and what should be reconsidered.
3. Next instructional step: what one concrete change should be introduced in the next lesson; how it will be implemented; what observable signs will show that it worked.

### Base Kazakh version

1. **Оқушылардың оқу мақсатына жеткенін қандай нақты жауаптар, әрекеттер немесе жұмыс нәтижелері дәлелдейді?**  
   **Қай оқушылар мақсатқа толық немесе ішінара жетпеді және оған қандай кедергілер әсер етті?**

2. **Сабақтағы оқыту тәсілдері, саралау, кері байланыс, сынып ортасы және уақытты ұйымдастыру оқушылардың белсенділігі мен оқуына қалай ықпал етті?**  
   **Қай тәсіл тиімді болды, ал қай тұсты қайта қарастыру қажет?**

3. **Келесі сабақта оқушылардың нәтижесін жақсарту үшін қандай бір нақты өзгеріс енгіземін?**  
   **Бұл өзгерісті қалай жүзеге асырамын және оның тиімді болғанын қандай белгілер арқылы анықтаймын?**

### Base Russian version

1. Какие конкретные ответы, действия или продукты учащихся доказывают достижение цели обучения? Кто достиг цели полностью или частично, и какие барьеры повлияли на результат?
2. Как методы обучения, дифференциация, обратная связь, среда класса и организация времени повлияли на активность и обучение? Что было эффективно, а что следует пересмотреть?
3. Какое одно конкретное изменение будет внесено в следующий урок, как оно будет реализовано и по каким признакам будет оценена его эффективность?

### Base English version

1. What specific learner responses, actions, or products demonstrate attainment of the learning objective? Which learners attained it fully or partially, and what barriers affected the result?
2. How did teaching approaches, differentiation, feedback, classroom environment, and time organization affect learner engagement and learning? What worked and what needs reconsideration?
3. What one concrete change will be introduced in the next lesson, how will it be implemented, and what evidence will show that it worked?

### Adaptation examples

- For mathematics, refer to solution strategies, reasoning, errors, and transfer to a new problem.
- For languages, refer to comprehension, speaking, writing, vocabulary, and language support.
- For science, refer to hypotheses, observations, data, experimental procedure, and conclusions.
- For history and social sciences, refer to sources, evidence, argumentation, chronology, and interpretation.
- For a class with language barriers, ask separately about the effect of vocabulary or sentence frames.
- For a large or multigrade class, ask about participation of different groups and time distribution.

## Processing the answers

After receiving the teacher's answers:
- separate evidence from impressions;
- use Form 2 of `lesson-quality-standard.docx` only for criteria supported by learner responses, actions, decisions, products, corrected errors, changed teacher support, or progress from the starting level;
- if the teacher requests scoring, use the 12 criteria on the `0-3` scale and mark unsupported criteria as `insufficient evidence` rather than guessing;
- identify attained and unfinished learning;
- identify effective and ineffective design choices;
- record the attained result, groups needing support, main difficulty, effective method or scaffold, and one testable next-lesson change;
- update the pedagogical-memory record;
- present a short summary for confirmation;
- ask the teacher: «Какой методический вопрос вы хотите разобрать перед следующим уроком?» in the appropriate language;
- if the teacher asks a methodological question, follow `socratic-dialogue.md`;
- store only the confirmed practical decision in pedagogical memory.

Before the next lesson, use the record to preserve effective elements, correct weaknesses, target support, and avoid method repetition.
